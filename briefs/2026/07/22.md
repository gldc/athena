# AI/Tech Brief — July 22, 2026

## TL;DR
- **DeepMind** launched Gemini 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, and 3.5 Flash Cyber models.
- **Anthropic** reached a $1.5B settlement regarding pirated books used in Claude training data.
- **Claude Code** capped its subagent concurrency and added emoji autocomplete in version 2.1.217.

## Key stories
- **Gemini 3.6 Flash and 3.5 Flash Cyber Released:** Google DeepMind announced new Gemini models, including a "Cyber" variant optimized for cybersecurity tasks, expanding their lightweight model lineup. [Source](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-6-flash-3-5-flash-lite-3-5-flash-cyber/)
- **Anthropic $1.5B Copyright Settlement:** A judge approved Anthropic's $1.5B settlement over the use of pirated books for training Claude, a major milestone in AI copyright litigation. [Source](https://apnews.com/article/ai-anthropic-copyright-settlement-claude-books-bartz-74b140444023898aeba8579b6e9f0d63)
- **Claude Code v2.1.217 Limits Subagents:** Anthropic updated Claude Code to restrict subagents from spawning nested subagents and capped concurrency at 20 to prevent unbounded fan-out. [Source](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/changelog)
- **OpenAI and Hugging Face Security Incident:** The two organizations addressed a security vulnerability discovered during model evaluation, highlighting the risks of code execution in model testing. [Source](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48997548)
- **Advertise in ChatGPT:** OpenAI appears to be preparing or launching an advertising platform within ChatGPT, signaling a shift toward ad-supported monetization. [Source](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48996571)
- **Jack Dorsey Launches Buzz:** The Block founder introduced "Buzz", combining team chat, AI agents, and Git hosting into a single platform. [Source](https://runtimewire.com/article/jack-dorsey-block-buzz-team-chat-ai-agents-git)

## Quiet but interesting

- **Kimi K3 Competes with Fable:** Fireworks AI reported that Kimi K3 is now competitive with Fable, suggesting shifts in the SOTA landscape for open models. [Source](https://fireworks.ai/blog/kimik3-fable)
- **Claude Solves 87-Year-Old Math Problem:** Superhuman reported that Claude reportedly cracked a long-standing math problem. [Source](https://www.superhuman.ai/)
