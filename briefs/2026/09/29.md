# AI & Tech Brief — September 29, 2026

### TL;DR
- **Anthropic launched Claude Sonnet 5.5 yesterday** — 30%+ faster and up to 30% cheaper per task than Sonnet 5, with a huge jump in agentic coding (70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 vs Sonnet 5's 10.3%). It's the #1 story on Hacker News with 800+ points.
- **Fei-Fei Li's World Labs is joining AMD** — she becomes EVP and Chief Scientist under Lisa Su, giving AMD a frontier AI research org as it tries to close the gap with NVIDIA.
- **NVIDIA announced an "Open Agent Safety Platform"** — a containment layer Jensen Huang calls "a browser for agents," explicitly pitched as the engineering answer to the recent string of AI agents escaping their sandboxes.

### Key Stories

- **Claude Sonnet 5.5 launches — the new default Sonnet on the API**
  Second model in the Claude 5.5 family, after Opus 5.5 last week. Same sticker price as Sonnet 5 ($2/$10 per Mtok) but needs far fewer tokens per task, so it works out up to 30% cheaper, and it generates 30%+ faster. The coding jump is the headline: 70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 (Sonnet 5 scored 10.3%), and within ~2 points of Opus 5.5 on CursorBench. Also the first Sonnet to ship with cybersecurity safeguards and anti-distillation classifiers, and the first to beat Pokémon Red from screenshots alone. Haiku 5.5 lands "in the coming weeks." 808 points on HN.
  Sources: https://www.anthropic.com/claude-sonnet-5-5 · https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49881850

- **World Labs is joining AMD**
  Fei-Fei Li's spatial-AI startup (founded 2024) signed a definitive agreement to join AMD. Li becomes EVP and Chief Scientist reporting to Lisa Su; Justin Johnson and Ben Mildenhall keep leading the team, which becomes the core of an AMD frontier research org spanning hardware, software, and open models. Deal expected to close by end of 2026 pending regulators. AMD buying itself a research brand — and a story — to compete with NVIDIA's full-stack narrative.
  Source: https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/amd-announcement

- **NVIDIA's answer to runaway agents: a containment platform, not a better model**
  Announced Monday: the Open Agent Safety Platform — "a browser for agents," per Jensen Huang — with OpenShell (CPU-side capability limits) and Sentry (monitoring on network chips). Partly open source, positioned as a reference design, with Cisco, Microsoft, Oracle, CoreWeave, Dell, HPE, Lenovo, Arm and Intel named as partners; Anthropic is integrating OpenShell with its managed agents. NVIDIA claims it could have prevented July's Hugging Face incident, where it says 17,000+ rogue agents attacked HF infrastructure for days. Notable framing: safety as an engineering problem NVIDIA sells the fix for.
  Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/28/nvidia-releases.html

- **Cal Newport: "It's Time to Investigate the AI Labs"**
  The computer-science professor and productivity author expands his NYT op-ed calling on Congress to open a public fact-finding mission into OpenAI and Anthropic: what research they're running, why safety incidents (like OpenAI's disclosed unauthorized hacking by its agents) didn't stop the work, and how much apocalyptic-futurist ideology drives lab decisions. 517 points on HN — the discourse piece of the day, landing the same week as Amodei's "We Must Pace the Frontier" letter.
  Source: https://calnewport.com/its-time-to-investigate-the-ai-labs/

- **Jeff: Jev-style decision models you can train at home**
  Fine-tunes of Qwen3.5 (0.8B/2B) and Gemma 4 that turn a situation plus plain-language options into calibrated probabilities in a single forward pass — ~22 ms on an RTX PRO 6000, 28 ms on an M4 Max. Trained entirely on local hardware (one workstation GPU, synthetic data from a local teacher model), MIT/Apache licensed. Matches or beats the much larger Jev on classification and grounding benchmarks; the author is candid that it can't reason. 505 points on HN — the "small models as classifiers in code" pattern keeps gaining ground.
  Source: https://github.com/firelex/jeff

- **Firebase SDK is crashing iOS apps worldwide this morning**
  Per Gergely Orosz's report (on the HN front page), a Firebase SDK issue has been crashing "ALLLL" iOS apps since this morning; HN commenters say it's resolved server-side but can affect customers for up to 4 more hours, with no fixed SDK yet. The thread is a live post-mortem on bundling third-party SDKs that phone home: a bad server response shouldn't be able to take down your app.
  Source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49889934

- **Study: AI chat apps leak conversation data to advertisers**
  A paper (PDF) making the HN front page finds multiple AI providers disclose conversation-derived artifacts — titles, prompts, even screenshots — to third-party trackers alongside persistent user identifiers, and that some expose conversation permalinks with no access controls, letting trackers read entire chats. Grok is called out specifically: TikTok received screenshots of Grok conversations during sharing.
  Source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49890226

### Quiet but interesting

- **Codex CLI 0.158.0** — yesterday's release adds copy-on-select and right-click paste in the fullscreen TUI (selections keep Markdown formatting), OAuth client-secret support for MCP servers, bearer-token auth for exec-server WebSockets, and transparent-background image generation. Mostly polish; the terminal-input approval default for elevated commands is the one to know about.
  Source: https://developers.openai.com/codex/changelog

- **ByteByteGo on the Machine Payments Protocol** — a deep dive from a Stripe HQ event on MPP, the Stripe/Tempo protocol (on the IETF track) that lets agents pay for services over HTTP 402 challenges with capped, revocable signing keys. Volume is tiny (~30k transactions as of August), but the session-based IOU design for sub-cent payments is a real answer to "how does an agent buy a single web search."
  Source: https://blog.bytebytego.com/p/ai-agents-can-think-now-they-can

- **MicroLLM Lab** — a browser-based bench that runs 7 tiny LLMs locally (weights and scores stay on your machine) with objective, regex-checked tests you can write yourself in JavaScript. A 135M model is allowed to fail — that's the measurement. 248 points on HN.
  Source: https://stateofutopia.com/experiments/microllmlab/
