- **Goldman Sachs restricts Claude Code and GitHub to engineers**
  State of AI, citing Bloomberg: internal guidance updated in October bars traders and bankers from the AI coding tools they had been using to track client flows, model market moves, and build written material; "strats" keep access. CIO Marco Argenti was publicly praising trader "vibe coding" as recently as July. The bank barred its Hong Kong bankers from Claude in April — and is a lead underwriter on Anthropic's IPO. The top HN comment frames the reversal as the lethal trifecta: semi-unattended agents on workstations holding client data, email, internet, and internal network access. **Why it matters:** the first big-bank rollback of the "everyone codes" era; expect every regulated industry to study the engineers-only line. (793 points, 400+ comments.)
  Source: https://www.thestateofai.com/news/goldman-sachs-claude-code-traders-bankers · Discussion: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50415628

- **"Is Opus 5.5 nerfed?" — users report Claude behavior shifts since November 3**
  A 234-point HN thread, prompted by a tweet tracking changed thinking patterns, is full of power users reporting degraded Opus 5.5 and Fable quality over the past one to two weeks — one long-horizon automation workflow reportedly dropped from ~85% to ~10% success.   Discussion: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50422069

- **Outlook.com is rejecting email from domains without DMARC**
  MailRoute reports that since November 5, Microsoft's consumer mail services (outlook.com, hotmail.com, live.com, msn.com) have been turning away some email from domains that publish no DMARC record — hitting ordinary low-volume senders, not just bulk mailers, with no announcement. Bounces read "550 5.7.1 Message rejected: Message lacks SPF/DKIM/DMARC". The fix is a single TXT record, `v=DMARC1; p=none`, which changes nothing about how other receivers treat your mail. **Why it matters:** DMARC just became effectively mandatory for the Microsoft consumer ecosystem overnight; audit any domain you send from today. (426 points.)
  Source: https://mailroute.net/blog/outlook-rejecting-email-without-dmarc

- **llama-modchip: a 284B DeepSeek-V4-Flash on a 64GB CPU box, no GPU**
  A new open-source project (644 stars) runs DeepSeek-V4-Flash at 9.1–14.7 tok/s generation with 256K context in under 59GiB RAM, plus 30B MoEs at 25–111 tok/s, on pure CPU. **Why it matters:** flagship-class MoE inference keeps collapsing onto commodity hardware — a 64GB workstation now runs what needed a GPU cluster eighteen months ago, which changes the math for on-prem and privacy-sensitive deployments. (432 points.)
