Live on QwenCloud (Token Plan or API), QwenWork, Qoder, and QwenStudio. **Why it matters:** price-performance pressure on the frontier keeps intensifying, and "Max is only the first look at Qwen4" — smaller siblings are explicitly coming to open source. - **One-click universal XSS in uBlock Origin (fixed)**
  A malicious website could silently drive uBlock Origin's element picker (Firefox MV2 version) into installing arbitrary *trusted* filters — which can inject JavaScript — achieving code execution in the context of any site with one tricked click. The extension's random internal UUID leaks through uBO's own fetch-hooking filter rules; from there it's an iframe, a postMessage, and a newline-injected filter. GPT-5.6 Sol did most of the discovery work; the human verified it. Fixed now. **Why it matters:** the web's most trusted extension had a universal-XSS path, and the AI-found / human-verified pipeline is now producing real high-severity bugs. (413 HN points.)
  Source: https://iter.ca/post/ubo-uxss/ · HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50513160

- **Old Bitly links hijacked in Burger King, McDonald's, and Trump tweets**
  Someone re-registered the "back-halves" of dead Bitly links from 2009–2013 posts: a 2011 Burger King tweet's restaurant-locator link led to Wikipedia's list of Nazi concentration camps, a McDonald's link to the 9/11 article, and 7 of 97 sampled Trump-tweet links elsewhere — some to crypto-lookalike domains. Bitly confirmed "bad actors" repurposed old inactive links, suspended the accounts, and is disabling affected links; CNN reports old posts from Gates, Trudeau, Pence, the FBI, and DoD were hit too. Bitly's own help pages claim back-halves "cannot be re-used." **Why it matters:** broken-link hijacking turns a verified account's old post into a phishing vector. Audit your org's old short links today (append `+` to a bit.ly link to preview its destination and creation date). (308 HN points.)
  Source: https://ransomnews.com/burger-king-tweet-bitly-link-hijack-2026/ · HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50511395

- **Microsoft 365 outage leads to chlorine gas poisoning of 15 hotel guests**
  During Friday's Microsoft outage, the Phantasialand resort's Hotel Ling Bao stopped receiving email — including the alarm its pool-safety system had already sent when the automatic dosing system over-chlorinated the water. Nobody saw the warning; 15 guests reported respiratory irritation, four went to hospital, one was kept for observation. **Why it matters:** a cloud email outage is now officially a physical-safety event. If your safety-of-life alarm path terminates in an inbox, this is your case study for redesigning it. (569 HN points.)
  Source: https://www1.wdr.de/nrw/rheinland/rhein-erft-kreis/technischer-defekt-chlor-phantasialand-100.html

- **Tor Project ships emergency DoS-defense release mid-DDoS**
  Tor 0.4.9.17 is a rapid-response release to a sustained DDoS attack hitting most relay operators — a massive flood of client circuits — with a key fix for relay DoS defenses around IPv6 handling. Relay operators should upgrade immediately; more releases are expected. **Why it matters:** the anonymity network is under active attack, and this is the first of a signaled series of countermeasure releases. (169 HN points.)
  Source: https://forum.torproject.org/t/security-release-0-4-9-17/22454 · HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50512441
