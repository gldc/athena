### Key Stories

- **Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO; stock skyrockets 31%**
  Nvidia's board voted unanimously to bring Huang back as President, CEO, and board member, citing "input from senior levels of the United States government" and confidence his return "poses no national security risk." Trump told CNBC he had pressed the board "directly and indirectly" and that Huang "did nothing wrong." Huang resigned earlier this month after reporting on extramarital affairs, including one with a woman the NYT says was in contact with China's Ministry of State Security; the stock had shed ~33% during the saga. The board's Wachtell investigation continues, and Huang is still due for House testimony Dec 1 and a public Senate Banking hearing Dec 3. **Why it matters:** the US government is now openly in the business of picking chip-company leadership — and the export-control questions (Megaspeed, Southeast Asia chip flows) are unresolved.
  Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-jensen-huang-ceo.html · HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50611576

- **Riemann Hypothesis: Claude-assisted proof certified in Lean**
  On Nov 20, Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge posted a four-page note claiming that, working with Claude, he had proved the Riemann Hypothesis (and the generalized form) — released only as a 17M-line Lean library, since a written-out version would run 250,000+ pages. A day later, a joint statement endorsed by the Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative certified that the library states and proves Mathlib's own `RiemannHypothesis`: dozens reproduced the compile, MIT's Andrew Sutherland built all 175,187 modules with zero errors and only Lean's three standard axioms, and independent proof checkers accepted the export. A rival all-human GRH draft by Alessandro Masullo, rushed out the same day by AMS president Ravi Vakil, remains unverified. **Why it matters:** the first Millennium-scale result where the open question is no longer "is it correct" but "can anyone extract the insight" — mathematics just crossed the machine-proof Rubicon.
  Sources: https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf · https://officechai.com/ai/lean-community-certifies-levent-alpoges-claude-assisted-proof-of-the-riemann-hypothesis-as-correct/

- **Ladybird browser ships its first alpha**
  The Ladybird project — a browser with its own engine written from scratch, not a Chromium/WebKit fork — released downloadable alphas for macOS and Linux. It's a rolling channel with automatic updates, WebDriver and Firefox DevTools protocol support for testing, and an explicit invitation for security researchers to poke holes. The team is upfront: some sites won't work and it will crash. **Why it matters:** the first from-scratch browser engine in decades to reach downloadable binaries — a real, if early, alternative to the Chromium monoculture.
  Source: https://ladybird.org/posts/alpha/

- **Stalwart 1.2.0: a drop-in, open-source replacement for Microsoft Exchange**
  The AGPL-licensed mail server now speaks MAPI over HTTP, Exchange Web Services, ActiveSync, and Autodiscover — Outlook, Apple Mail, and phone apps connect exactly as they would to Exchange, calendars and contacts included. The timing is deliberate: Exchange 2016/2019 left support, Microsoft's paid ESU reportedly ended in October, the new Outlook refuses on-premises accounts, and EWS in Exchange Online starts switching off this year. **Why it matters:** for organizations that can't or won't put their mail in a US cloud, the "users won't leave Outlook" objection to leaving Exchange just died.
  Source: https://stalw.art/blog/microsoft-exchange-replacement/

- **Codex CLI 0.172.0 (today)**
  Today's release adds thread links from search results and transcripts into saved conversations and nested agents, Tab-completion for slash-command arguments, Shift+Up/Down navigation between task groups in the agent command center, per-tool approval reviewers for connected apps, and Amazon Bedrock Runtime selection in app-server setup. Fixes cover `codex exec resume` restoring sandbox permissions and cross-OS remote sessions. **Why it matters:** steady convergence on the "mission control for parallel agents" shape — resume fidelity was the missing piece for long-running unattended work.
  Source: https://developers.openai.com/codex/changelog

- **Claude Code's Nov 23 release**
  Code Mode now works in terminal sessions on Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud's Agent Platform, Microsoft Foundry, and Claude Platform on AWS; `claude doctor` gains an API connectivity check; plugin manifests support `optionalDependencies`; and a large VSCode batch lands (notebook "Add Cell to Chat", "Fix with Claude Code" quick fixes, "Add to Chat" from Source Control, merge-conflict resolution). One heads-up: on or after Nov 30, managed plugin marketplaces in `extraKnownMarketplaces` will auto-update by default — set `"autoUpdate": false` to opt out. **Why it matters:** Code Mode on the big three clouds removes a real enterprise blocker, and the marketplace auto-update change is a supply-chain setting admins should decide on this week.
  Source: https://code.claude.com/docs/en/changelog

- **OpenAI launches OpenWorks for small businesses**
  Per today's Superhuman edition, OpenAI's new OpenWorks pilot pays AI experts a stipend of up to $8,000/month to help independently owned small businesses — restaurants, retailers, service firms across 20 US communities — put AI to work; software, venture-backed, and PE-owned businesses are excluded. **Why it matters:** a subsidized last-mile adoption play — OpenAI is paying humans to close the gap between model capability and Main Street usage.
  Source: https://www.superhuman.ai/p/jensen-s-back-while-openai-matches-ai-experts-with-business-owners
