### Key Stories

- **Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO**
  Huang resigned Thursday after NYT reporting tied his affair with Megaspeed's Alice Huang to a suspected Chinese influence operation, and Reuters added a second alleged affair with a subordinate. Nvidia named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO and its first-ever chairman (Harvey C. Jones); it has no succession plan, and earnings land Tuesday. The stock opened down 14% and closed down ~4%; the FBI is investigating and lawmakers want Huang under oath. **Why it matters:** the CEO most synonymous with the AI boom is out, and the national-security framing darkens Nvidia's years of lobbying to loosen chip export controls on China. (3,640 HN points.)

- **Anthropic files for IPO**
  Anthropic's IPO site is live: the company has filed a registration statement (including a U.S. prospectus) with the SEC — not yet effective — plus an amended preliminary base PREP prospectus with Canadian securities commissions. **Why it matters:** the first frontier lab to formally file for the public market; the coming disclosures should be the clearest look yet at frontier-AI economics. (953 HN points.)
  Source: https://ipo.anthropic.com/us/ · HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50465829

- **Global Microsoft 365 outage hits day two**
  Outlook/Exchange Online, Teams, and Copilot have been degraded or down since Thursday — reports hit 74 countries within an hour. Banks, city governments, courts, NHS prescription workflows, and UK police online forms all took the hit; one company postponed its earnings call. Microsoft EVP Charles Lamanna says there's "no indication" of malicious or cyber activity; Australia's home affairs minister said Microsoft likened it to CrowdStrike — an update gone awry. The ripple reached AI too: OpenAI reported issues with Microsoft connectors in ChatGPT. **Why it matters:** a live stress test of how much of the world's work runs through one vendor. (962 HN points on the main thread.)
  Source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/12/business/live-news/microsoft-global-outage-intl-hnk · https://status.cloud.microsoft/ · HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50471731

- **Gemini 4 Hydrogen ships in Antigravity, with a new "Orchestrator" mode**
  Google is rolling out Gemini 4 Hydrogen today inside Antigravity: a fast everyday coding model (51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, 57% on Terminal-Bench 4.1 — triple Gemini 3.8 Flash's 18.2%) with four thinking levels including a new `xhigh`. The bigger idea is Orchestrator, which hands a single task back and forth between Hydrogen and frontier models like Gemini 4 Carbon mid-trajectory instead of statically routing up front — Google claims near-Carbon success at 24% less quota and 19% faster. **Why it matters:** model routing is moving inside the agent loop, where a task's hard parts are discovered rather than guessed from your first prompt. (426 HN points.)
  Source: https://antigravity.google/blog/gemini-4-hydrogen-in-google-antigravity/ · HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50472260

- **Rust 1.100: the never type is stable after 10 years**
  Headline: `!` can finally be named anywhere and `Infallible` becomes an alias for it (small breaking change to never-type fallback in pre-2024 editions). Also stabilized: the `Allocator` trait for custom allocators on `Vec`/`Box`, `DropGuard` for run-on-exit closures, unused-dependency warnings in Cargo, and — the sleeper feature — `min-publish-age`, Cargo-native dependency cooldowns that skip package versions published in, say, the last 3 days. **Why it matters:** cooldowns put a real supply-chain-attack defense one config line away in a major package manager. (720 HN points.)
  Source: https://blog.rust-lang.org/2026/11/12/Rust-1.100.0/ · HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50472038

- **TSMC discloses employee-data leak in an SEC filing**
  A 6-K dated November 12 says some current and former employee data "was made available on an external website without authorization," verified to match data held by a cloud service provider — with "no evidence that TSMC's systems have been compromised" and no link to operations or customer data. **Why it matters:** read past HN's "TSMC Hacked" headline — this is third-party cloud exposure of HR data, not a fab breach. (102 HN points.)
  Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000104617926000800/a20261112statementregardin.htm · HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50479721

- **Brave Search API now takes per-request payments from agents**
  Brave added x402 support to its Search API: no account or API key, just an HTTP 402 with a price, a signed USDC payment on Base, and results with an on-chain receipt — $0.005 per query, gas covered for a limited time. The suggested wallet is Purl, a Stripe-developed CLI wallet for agents. **Why it matters:** agentic commerce plumbing is standardizing fast — the same week United moved to ban booking agents, one of the largest independent search indexes made itself natively payable by machines. (129 HN points.)
  Source: https://brave.com/blog/search-api-agentic-payments/ · HN: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50477649
