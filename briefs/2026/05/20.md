# AI/Tech Brief — 2026-05-20

### TL;DR
- Google unveiled Gemini 3.5 Flash and announced the sunset of the Gemini CLI in favor of the new Antigravity CLI.
- OpenAI expanded its ecosystem with Realtime 2 voice models, streaming translation, and a preview of Sora 2.
- Andrej Karpathy has officially joined Anthropic, marking a significant talent shift in the AI landscape.

### Key stories
- **Gemini 3.5 & CLI Sunset:** Google debuted Gemini 3.5 Flash and scheduled the Gemini CLI for deprecation on June 18, 2026. This forces developers to migrate to the Google Antigravity 2.0 platform. ([Google Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-5/) / [Dev Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/an-important-update-transitioning-gemini-cli-to-antigravity-cli/))
- **Anthropic Agent View:** Claude Code introduced "Agent View" and persistent background sessions. This enables more autonomous, long-running agent workflows directly from the command line. ([Source](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/changelog))
- **OpenAI Voice & Codex:** OpenAI rolled out Realtime 2 with streaming translation and updated Codex for Mac-to-mobile connectivity. These updates dramatically reduce latency for voice applications and remote coding. ([API](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog) / [Codex](https://developers.openai.com/codex/changelog))
- **Sora 2 Preview:** OpenAI launched a follow-up to Sora, featuring high character consistency and granular copyright controls. It paves the way for licensed, interactive AI-generated media. ([Source](https://blog.samaltman.com/sora-2))
- **Karpathy Joins Anthropic:** The high-profile AI researcher announced his move to Anthropic. This consolidation of talent could significantly accelerate Anthropic's pursuit of interpretable superintelligence. 

### Quiet but interesting
- **The Gentle Singularity:** Sam Altman published an essay arguing that society has already passed the event horizon for digital superintelligence, pointing toward near-term abundance. ([Source](https://blog.samaltman.com/the-gentle-singularity))
- **DeepMind Science Initiatives:** DeepMind deployed Co-Scientist and WeatherNext, pushing multi-agent architectures into rigorous scientific research and climate prediction. ([Source](https://deepmind.google/blog/))
- **Gemini CLI Memory Inbox:** In its final major update (v0.42.0), the Gemini CLI introduced a new inbox flow for managing extracted agent skills. ([Source](https://geminicli.com/docs/changelogs/))
