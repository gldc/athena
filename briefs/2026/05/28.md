[Source](https://www.superhuman.ai/)

[Source](https://www.pcgamer.com/hardware/duckduckgos-ai-free-search-saw-nearly-28-percent-more-visits-in-the-week-following-googles-insistence-that-people-love-ai-mode/) | [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48296649)
